# Module 1 Project: AI Prompt Kit — Test Results

## Business Details

- **Business Name:** Bilal Tech Gadgets
- **Business Type:** Electronics shop
- **City:** Lahore
- **Target Customers:** Students and families
- **Monthly Marketing Budget:** PKR 15,000

## Test 1: Marketing Caption

**Model:** Gemma 3 1B (`gemma3:1b`)

### Prompt Tested

Write one short Instagram caption for Bilal Tech Gadgets in Lahore for students and families. Use a friendly tone. Maximum 60 words. Do not invent product availability, prices, or discounts. Output only the caption.

### Actual AI Output

Hey everyone! 👋 We're Bilal Tech Gadgets, purring with cool solutions for smart students and happy families in Lahore. Connecting you with awesome gadgets to explore, learn and have fun! 😎 Stay tuned - new things coming soon. 😊 # BilalTech Gadgets #Lahore #Tech #KidsTech

### Evaluation

- The caption uses a friendly tone and mentions the business location.
- It is short enough for social media.
- “Purring with cool solutions” sounds unnatural.
- It contains four hashtags, with awkward formatting.
- It does not specify prices or confirm particular products are in stock.
- Human editing is recommended before publishing.

## Test 2: Customer Service

**Model:** Gemma 3 1B (`gemma3:1b`)

### Prompt Tested

Write a polite customer service reply for Bilal Tech Gadgets in Lahore to a customer whose order arrived late. Apologize sincerely and offer a small discount on their next order, but do not invent order details. Keep it under 80 words. Output only the reply.

### Actual AI Output

Subject: Update regarding your Recent Order

Dear [Customer Name],

We're sincerely sorry to inform you that your order delivered later than expected. Please accept our heartfelt apologies for the inconvenience. We understand frustration. To show our appreciation for your patience, we would like to offer you a 10% discount code for your next purchase with us. We appreciate your understanding.

Sincerely,

Bilal Tech Gadgets Team

### Evaluation

- The response apologizes politely and acknowledges the delivery delay.
- The model invented a 10% discount that the business has not confirmed.
- `[Customer Name]` is a placeholder that needs to be replaced or removed.
- “We understand frustration” is grammatically awkward.
- The reply needs human review before being sent to a real customer.

## Test 3: Business Growth Ideas

**Model:** Gemma 3 1B (`gemma3:1b`)

### Prompt Tested

Suggest 3 practical, low-cost AI ideas for Bilal Tech Gadgets, an electronics shop in Lahore. Target customers are students and families. Monthly budget: PKR 15,000. Give each idea a short description and estimated cost in PKR. Label all costs as estimates, do not invent verified prices, and keep the answer under 150 words. Output only the 3 ideas.

### Actual AI Output

1. **AI-Suggested Product Combinations:** Use an ML model to suggest potential product bundles. Customers enter their needs (e.g., "book + laptop"), and the AI recommends fitting products, leveraging past sales data and popular bundles.  
   **Estimated Cost: PKR 500–1,500**

2. **Inventory & Trend Identification:** Implement a simple predictive model analyzing recent sales and local trends (like school supplies and holiday needs) using publicly available data. This helps alert the business about demand fluctuations and minimize lost inventory.  
   **Estimated Cost: PKR 7,000–10,000**

3. **Personalized Wish Lists:** Create a simple AI feature that offers relevant suggestions when a customer adds an item to their cart, based on past purchases or trending items.  
   **Estimated Cost: PKR 2,000–5,000**

### Evaluation

- The model generated three ideas relevant to an electronics business.
- The costs are estimates generated by AI and have not been independently verified.
- The first idea assumes usable sales data is available.
- Inventory prediction and personalized wish lists may require technical development and customer data.
- The estimated costs total PKR 9,500–16,500 if all three ideas are implemented. The upper end exceeds the PKR 15,000 monthly budget.
- A simpler starting point would be to use AI to help recommend products based on existing business information, after checking any implementation costs.

## Overall Reflection

Gemma 3 1B generated ideas for marketing, customer service, and business growth. However, the outputs were not perfect. The marketing caption contained unnatural wording, the customer-service reply invented an unconfirmed 10% discount, and the growth ideas included unverified cost estimates.

This project demonstrates that AI can support small-business tasks, but its outputs must be checked by a human before publication or use in business decisions.
